Privacy Policy

Morning Brief · Last updated 24 July 2026

Morning Brief is an internal reporting tool operated by Stelor Inc. for its own business use. It has no public users, no signups, and is not offered to third parties.

What it accesses

Morning Brief reads business data from systems operated by or on behalf of Stelor Inc. and Reserve Plus:

  • Microsoft 365 — mail and calendar
  • Slack — channel and direct-message history
  • Internal dashboards and spreadsheets
  • QuickBooks Online — account balances and deposit/withdrawal totals

Access to QuickBooks Online is read-only. The tool does not create, modify, or delete records in QuickBooks Online.

How it is used

Content is summarized into a periodic briefing delivered to a private Slack channel visible only to authorized personnel. It is used for internal situational awareness and business reporting, and for no other purpose.

Third-party processing

To produce summaries, content is transmitted to Anthropic's API for processing. That data is not used to train models.

What we do not do

  • Data is not sold, rented, or licensed to third parties.
  • Data is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Data is not used to build advertising or behavioural profiles.

Retention and security

Generated briefings and operational logs are held in a private, access-controlled store for internal reference and are deleted when no longer needed. Credentials and access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never committed to source control.
