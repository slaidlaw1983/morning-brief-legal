Connecting to QuickBooks Online

Morning Brief · Stelor Inc.

Morning Brief is an internal reporting tool operated by Stelor Inc. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Connecting or reconnecting a QuickBooks Online company is performed by an authorized administrator from the command line:

brief qbo-auth

This opens the Intuit consent screen, and on approval stores a refresh token locally so scheduled briefings can read account balances and deposit and withdrawal totals. Access is read-only.

If a connection lapses or is revoked, an administrator re-runs the same command to restore it.
