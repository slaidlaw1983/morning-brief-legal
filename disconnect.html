Disconnecting from QuickBooks Online

Morning Brief · Stelor Inc.

To disconnect Morning Brief from a QuickBooks Online company:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an administrator.
  2. Go to Settings → Apps (or Manage your apps).
  3. Locate Morning Brief and choose Disconnect.

Once disconnected, the stored refresh token is no longer valid and Morning Brief can no longer read from that company. Any locally stored token for the company is deleted, and no further data is retrieved.

To reconnect afterwards, see Connecting to QuickBooks Online.
